The world

Toronto Beauty brings one aesthetics standard to a network of providers across the Greater Toronto Area.

Every provider in the network looks and speaks to the same mark.

The rules of this world

  • CPSO and CNORules for the physicians and nurses who prescribe and deliver treatments.
  • Health CanadaPrescription drugs cannot be promoted to the public by brand name.
  • Competition ActNo false or misleading claims, and pricing that shows the full cost.

    Who you are, who you stand against, and what you are called. The brief and the codex every other layer reads from.

  2. 2

    Law

    What you can say, what you can charge, and how it is packaged. Offers and claims checked against your regulator.

    Site, search and AI visibility. Where people find your world and how they enter it.

  6. 6

  7. 7

    Machinery

    Intake, booking, follow-up and reporting. The systems that keep the world running without you.
