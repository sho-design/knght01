The world

Black Lotus Coffee House is a coffee brand rooted in Vietnamese tradition and wellness, built for high performers and conscious drinkers.

KNGHT's founder was on the founding team. Born in Da Nang, the brand is now based in Toronto, importing into Canada and selling wholesale and retail.

What changed

The brand grew from online sales into retail in Da Nang, and is now based in Toronto, importing into Canada and selling wholesale and retail. It is known for holding cultural roots and modern wellness in one mark.
