Privacy · Last updated 1 October 2026

What we keep,
and why.

KNGHT is a brand studio in Toronto, Ontario. We collect as little as we can, we use it only to answer you and do the work, and we never sell it. This page follows Canada's federal privacy law, PIPEDA, and Canada's anti-spam law, CASL.

What we collect

  • When you book a callYour name, email, the time you choose and anything you write in the booking questions, including a self-check score if you took one. Booking runs through Calendly, which stores these details for us.
  • When you ask for your scorecard by emailYour email address, your seven self-check answers and your score, plus your consent to hear from us. This field only appears when email delivery is switched on.
  • When you take the self-checkNothing leaves your browser unless you choose to send it. Downloading your scorecard creates the image on your own device.
  • When you email usWhatever you choose to send to sho@knght.com.
  • On your deviceTwo small notes in your browser's storage: the category you picked, so the page reads for you next time, and whether you have seen the intro, so it does not replay. No advertising or tracking cookies.

Why we use it

To confirm and hold your call, to send what you asked for, and to prepare for and deliver work you hire us to do.

If you tick the box, we may also send occasional notes from KNGHT. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and we stop the day you use it.

Who else sees it

  • CalendlyHolds booking details and sends confirmations and reminders.
  • Our email providerDelivers and stores email we send and receive.
  • Our form serviceReceives scorecard requests, once email delivery is switched on.
  • Hosting and mediaVercel serves this site. Images and films load from a content delivery network, and fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website, these services see your IP address and browser type when they deliver files.

Some of these providers store data outside Canada, including in the United States, where it is subject to local law. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not use it to train AI models.

How long we keep it

Booking and enquiry details: up to 24 months after our last contact, unless you become a client.

Client records: as long as the engagement lasts, plus the period tax and accounting law requires.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email sho@knght.com and we will answer within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca.

Who is responsible

Sergio Ho, founder, is accountable for personal information at KNGHT. Questions go to sho@knght.com.

If we change this page in a way that matters, we will change the date at the top.
